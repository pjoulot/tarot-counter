Tarot Points keeps score at French Tarot, deal after deal, following the French Tarot Federation (FFT) rules. No calculator, no scribbled sheet: pick the taker and the contract, enter the points, and the app does the rest.

For 3, 4 or 5 players
• Prise, garde, garde sans, garde contre
• At 5, the called king: taker, partner and defenders each get their own share
• Petit au bout, poignées, chelem announced or not
• Quick entry ("made by…", "failed by…"), by points, or card by card when nobody wants to count
• "Everyone passes" in one tap

The score sheet
• Ranking, deal-by-deal table or score chart
• Tap a deal to see how it was scored, fix it or delete it
• Open-ended game, a set number of deals, or up to a target score
• At the end: podium, highlights, sharing, and a rematch with the same table

Your players and your stats
• Saved players, with an animal, a colour or their own photo
• Wins, points, takes made by contract, best deal, best partner
• Over all games or the last 30 days

Your way
• Round scores to the nearest 10, choose who deals after a pass
• Misère d'atout and de tête as a house rule, off by default
• The tarot rules on one page, to settle arguments
• Light or dark theme, in English or French
• Backup export and import, spreadsheet export (CSV)

Free, no ads, no account
Your games stay on your phone: nothing is sent anywhere, and the app works offline.

Tarot Points is made by Philippe, in his spare time.
